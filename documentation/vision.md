## Product Vision  
**For** all individuals in the San Luis Obispo Area **who** would like to sell or give away 
clothes. The clothes app is an online clothes listing site **that** allows users to post and
view local clothes listings in their surrounding area. **Unlike** Facebook Marketplace, you
don't need to filter unwanted products or view repetitive ads. **Ours** is only designed for
the selling and buying of clothes and we will never pester you with ads.
